# Story Playbook

How to make Instagram stories in your brand. Process: skill `story-create`.

## Format
- **1080×1920 (9:16)**, 24 hours of life, watched in seconds. Keep text and buttons inside the safe zone
  (tokens `safe.story`: default top 250 px, bottom 1600 px of 1920).
- ≤ 15 words per frame. One idea, one action. Stories carry **no caption and no hashtags**.

## Shapes
- **Single:** cover / quote / stat + one action.
- **Sequence of 3–7:** hook → value (one fact per frame) → interaction (poll, quiz, question box) → CTA (link sticker or "watch the post").

## Native stickers (added in the app, not in the image)
Poll, quiz, question box, countdown, link. Leave a clear area for them; write down which sticker goes where.

## Use cases
Promote a post or video, teach a tip, ask the audience, share behind the scenes, sell. Save a great sequence to a Highlight.

## Publishing
Skill `buffer-publishing-setup`: story = **Story** type, one file at a time, no caption.

## Key Takeaways
- One screen, one idea, one action.
- Stay inside the safe zone; stickers are added in the app.
