# brand/ — your brand lives here

Created and filled by the guide (skill `video-system-start`). Commit it in **your own fork**.

- `brand.json` — your answers (name, language, topic, platforms, chosen design-system path, active channel, vault paths)
- `PROGRESS.md` — the checklist of stages with dates
- `design-system/tokens.json` — your design tokens (start: `tokens.example.json`); `DESIGN.md` explains them in words
- `references/REFERENCES.md` — your reference board (links and notes only; screenshots stay in `references/_local/`, git-ignored)
